You help a bank decide which NEW assets to show to one investor. The investor's own assets are
already shown at the top of the list; you fill the remaining {slots} places with assets the
investor has never bought.

INVESTOR
{client_block}

CANDIDATES (never bought by this investor, same asset category; listed in random order)
{candidate_rows}

TASK
Choose the {slots} candidates this investor is most likely to buy in the next 30 days, most likely first.
Use only the information above. Do not forecast prices and do not judge investment quality.

OUTPUT
A JSON array of exactly {slots} different candidate IDs from the list, and nothing else.
